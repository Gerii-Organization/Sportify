Delete your Sportify account

You can delete your account and everything in it, either from inside the app or by asking us.

From the app

  1. Open Sportify and go to the menu (top right of the home screen).
  2. Scroll to the bottom.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

By email

Write to [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL] from the address your account uses, with the subject "Delete my account". We will confirm and delete it within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Your profile and measurements, every workout and set you have logged, personal records, streaks, body weight history, logged meals, messages and group memberships, friendships, feed activity, and any images you uploaded.

Deletion is permanent. There is no recovery and no export afterwards — take a copy of anything you want to keep first.

What is kept, and for how long

Nothing is retained after deletion except where the law requires it. Encrypted backups are overwritten on a rolling basis and are fully cleared within 30 days.
