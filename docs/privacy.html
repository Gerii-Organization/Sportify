Health data

Sleep and step data read through Apple Health stays on your device except where it is summarised into your own daily totals, which are stored with your account so the app works across sessions. Workouts you finish in Sportify may be written back to Apple Health so they count towards your activity rings.

In line with Apple's requirements: Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, is never sold, and is never shared with third parties for their own purposes. You can revoke Health access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

Meal photos

When you photograph a meal, the image is sent to OpenAI through our server, which returns an estimated calorie and macronutrient breakdown. The estimate is saved with your account; the photo is used for that request and is not kept by us afterwards. OpenAI processes it under its API terms and does not use API data to train its models. If you would rather not send photos anywhere, enter your meals manually instead.

What other people can see

Other Sportify users can see your first name, profile photo, level, XP, streak, equipped cosmetic items, and workouts you have explicitly marked public. They cannot see your weight, height, age, sex, meals or private workouts. Blocking someone stops them seeing your profile and your public workouts.

Legal basis (GDPR)

We process your account and training data to perform the contract you enter into by using the app (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Crash reports and usage counts are processed on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it (Art. 6(1)(f)); they contain no health data and no message content. Health data, including sleep and body measurements, is a special category and is processed on the basis of your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a)), which you give in the app and in your phone's permission prompts, and which you can withdraw at any time by revoking the permission or deleting your account.
